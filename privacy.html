Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

PostPilot ("the app") is a free desktop app created by Pengkie Magno ("I", "me"), an individual developer. Contact: pengkiemgn@gmail.com.

The short version

PostPilot publishes the posts you write to the accounts you connect. Your content and your connections live on your own computer. I do not run a database of users, I do not sell or share data, and I do not show ads.

What information is involved

  • Content you create (titles, captions, image links, schedules). Stored only on your computer, in your browser's local storage.
  • Facebook / Instagram connection data you authorize through Meta's login: the names and IDs of the Facebook Pages you choose, the username and ID of any linked Instagram professional account, and access tokens that allow PostPilot to publish on your behalf. Tokens are saved in a private file on your own computer (folder .postpilot in your user folder).
  • Permissions requested: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish. They are used only to list your Pages/accounts and to publish posts that you create and schedule in the app.

The sign-in relay

To keep the app's secret key out of the downloadable app, a small online "relay" completes Meta's login. During sign-in the relay briefly receives your one-time login code and the resulting tokens and passes them straight back to your computer. It does not store, log or keep them. It is hosted on Cloudflare Workers; Cloudflare, as infrastructure provider, may process standard technical request data (such as IP address) under its own privacy policy.

What I do not do

  • I do not collect your Facebook or Instagram posts, messages, friends, followers or analytics.
  • I do not sell, rent or share your data, and I do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.
  • The app contains no analytics or tracking.

Sharing

Your posts and tokens are sent only to Meta (to publish what you asked to publish). Nothing is sent to anyone else.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your computer until you remove it. You can disconnect an account in PostPilot (Connections page), delete the .postpilot folder, and remove PostPilot from your Facebook settings (Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations). See how to delete your data.

Security

Tokens are stored in a file readable only by your user account, and the local connector accepts requests only from your own computer. No system is perfectly secure; protect your computer and sign-ins.

Children

PostPilot is not intended for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new date will appear above.
