Delete your data

PostPilot does not keep your data on any server, so there is no account to close online. Everything is on your own computer. To remove it:

  1. In PostPilot: open Connections and click Disconnect next to each Facebook / Instagram account. This deletes the saved access token.
  2. Delete local files (optional): delete the folder .postpilot in your user folder (for example C:\Users\YourName\.postpilot). To remove your posts, use Settings → Download backup if you want a copy, then clear PostPilot's site data in Chrome or uninstall the app.
  3. Remove access on Facebook: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or "Apps and websites") → select PostPilot → Remove. This revokes PostPilot's permission at Meta.

Requested through Meta's tools? If you removed PostPilot from Facebook and were sent here, your request is complete: the relay stores no personal data, so nothing remains to delete on my side. Your confirmation reference is shown in the page address as code=….
