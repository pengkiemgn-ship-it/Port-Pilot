PostPilot

Write once. Post to Facebook and Instagram at the same time. Free, with no subscription and no automation service in the middle.

Download for Windows

How it works

  1. Install Node.js (free), then extract PostPilot and run INSTALL-POSTPILOT.bat.
  2. Open PostPilot and click Connect Facebook & Instagram.
  3. Write your post, tick the accounts, schedule it. PostPilot publishes it for you.

Your data stays with you

Your posts and your account connections are stored on your own computer. PostPilot has no user accounts and no database. Read the privacy policy.
